What these numbers meanApple designs iPhone 14 and earlier batteries to retain 80% original capacity at around 500 cycles under ideal conditions; iPhone 15 and later at around 1,000. This is design guidance, not a definitive fault threshold.
Simple real-world observationsThese are guided checks, not electrical measurements. Do not connect a charger if the battery is swollen, damaged or unusually hot.
When safe, does iOS display a charging indication when connected?

A charging icon alone does not establish charging-port or battery electrical integrity.
