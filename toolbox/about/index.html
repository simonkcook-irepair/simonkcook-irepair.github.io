A Note from Simon

For over 13 years, I’ve been doing what I love — working with phones, helping people, and solving problems every single day.

Over that time, I’ve seen how difficult it can be for customers to understand what might actually be wrong with their iPhone. I’ve also seen how useful the right testing tools can be when they’re simple, honest and practical.

That’s why I developed iRepair Toolbox.

This app is built from real experience in the repair trade and is designed to help customers, fellow technicians, and anyone who wants to run useful checks on their iPhone.

It’s not about overpromising. It’s about giving people better guidance, better information, and tools that reflect the realities of diagnosing iPhone faults.

This app is an extension of the work I do every day through iRepair & Rescue Ltd — helping people get answers and helping devices get back to how they should be.

Simon CookFounder · iRepair & Rescue Ltd · Swansea, South Wales
